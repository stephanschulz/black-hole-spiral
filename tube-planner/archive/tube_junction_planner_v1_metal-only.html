How this is worked out
  • The path is taken from the metal body, not the camera scan. Every rib's top edge was re-read from its B-rep outline; 860 teeth came out, pitch 10.00 mm on all fifteen ribs. The rib numbers were read the same way — they are cut through the plate at r ≈ 115–150.
  • Where this still disagrees with your wall. At 1 tooth/turn the tooth NUMBER comes out right — rib 05's 6th crossing is its 6th tooth — but the arc to get there is 5.33 m against the 5.00 m your two tubes give, so the path here runs about 6 % long. At 2 teeth/turn the arc is closer but the tooth number is 11, not 6. Something in my radii or in the centre length is off; the rate control is there so you can set what you actually see rather than take my word.
  • Tooth #n followed around the ring gains 10 mm of radius per turn (straight-line fit, residual rms 0.6 mm). The teeth are one helix, not fifteen identical combs. Taking every second tooth gives a 20 mm/turn winding — the installed strip measures 20.0 mm/turn.
  • How far to trust a prediction. A 1 % scale error accumulates to a whole holder gap within a few turns, so joints far from the anchor are indicative, not exact. Re-anchor on the wall as you go — tell me the next joint you find and its rib and holder, and this re-fits.
