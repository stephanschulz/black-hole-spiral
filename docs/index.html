Every joint

A joint and its flat cable take up of tube, and a rib tooth is , so a joint clears only if its centre is more than from the rib. From tip is measured along the tube from its centre end, where the data stops. From rim counts teeth inward from the outermost tooth on that rib. Near the outside, that is the easier count to make. Marginal means it lies within the map's uncertainty: 1° of angle, about 7 mm at mid-radius.
